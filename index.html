What we work on in a playing lesson

Most good players don't lose strokes to their swing. They lose them to decisions. On the course, I'll watch three of yours.

Where you aim
On every tee shot and approach, I'll ask you one question: which target takes a double bogey out of play? It's rarely the flag.
What club you pull
Most golfers choose clubs by their best shot, not their average one. We'll match your clubs to your real carry distances, so short-sided misses disappear.
How you recover
When you're in trouble, the goal is one shot back to safety. I'll show you when to take your medicine and when the hero shot is actually the smart play.

Try this on your next round: before every shot, say your target out loud, and name the miss you can live with. You'll be surprised how many strokes that saves.
